COVERAGE AREA DETAIL DEMO

The percentages in js/coverage.js are illustrative sample values used only to demonstrate the professional coverage-area details UI. They are not measured or verified GREEN CITY NET coverage statistics.

Before publishing to customers, replace each `percent` value with verified data from your network/field survey, or remove the percentage indicator if no defensible measurement exists. Keep the explanatory note in coverage.html until values are verified.

Area cards expand to show area profile, services to enquire about, availability note, technology enquiry type, and a contact link prefilled with the area name.
