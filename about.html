WHO WE ARE

Internet that helps everyday life move forward.

GREEN CITY NET is an internet service provider brand based in Degerchala, Gazipur. Our focus is to make connectivity easier to access and simpler to manage, from everyday home browsing to the needs of growing businesses.

We believe good service means more than speed: it also means clear communication, thoughtful technical planning and support that understands local customers.
